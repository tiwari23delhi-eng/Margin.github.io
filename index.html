Photosynthesis: the short version

Biology · 4 min read

Plants turn light, water and CO₂ into glucose and oxygen.

Light reactions happen in the thylakoids; the Calvin cycle happens in the stroma.

Exam tip: always state where each stage takes place.

Memory trick

Follow the green

Light→Chlorophyll (green)→Water splits→Sugar made→O₂ out

Thylakoid Takes the light. Stroma Sugars it up.
